# Delete your Hayatuk account

You can delete your Hayatuk account and its associated data at any time. There
are two ways to do it.

## Option 1 — In the app (instant)

1. Open **Hayatuk** and sign in.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm in the dialog.

Your account and personal data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

## Option 2 — By email

If you can't access the app, email **contact@hayatuk.org** from the address
associated with your account, with the subject **"Delete my account"**. We will
verify ownership and delete your account within **30 days**.

## What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your personal data, including:

- Profile details: name, email, phone number, and blood type
- Your approximate location and region
- Blood requests you created
- Requests you accepted (your donation history)
- Your push-notification token

This action is **permanent and cannot be undone**.

## Data retention

Personal data is deleted as described above. We may retain a limited amount of
information only where required by law (for example, basic transaction or
security logs), for the minimum period required, after which it is also deleted.
No retained data is used to re-identify you or to contact you.
